How the weekly difficulty works

Monday has a direct bridge. Tuesday through Friday extend the chains from 2 to 5 words in the tested route. Saturday has a 7-word route with wider spacing and less common starting letters. Sunday returns to a gentle 2-word connection. Daily word choices, orientation, and positions also change.

Difficulty labels are estimates, not measured player success rates. Route length and target score describe one verified legal solution; clever players may find a shorter connection. The static generator repeats a limited family of patterns over time.

HOW SCORING WORKS

Make shorter connections for a better score.

Each accepted word costs 10 points + its letters. Starting words are free, invalid tries cost nothing, and lower scores win the bragging rights.

Game rules

  1. Connect Start A to Start B. Build outward from either starting word.
  2. Place a listed word left-to-right or top-to-bottom, entirely inside the grid.
  3. Ordinary words cross exactly once: the shared letter must match at a clean, perpendicular intersection. Words cannot overlap in the same direction or touch alongside one another.
  4. Finish with one bridge. The final word may cross exactly two existing words—one in each separate starting chain. No other word may make two crossings.
  5. Lower scores are better. An accepted word costs 10 points plus its length; invalid placements cost nothing.

Vocabulary note: this edition uses 359,039 bundled English words that fit the grid, including common words such as DILL. No list is every dictionary. Only alphabetic words of 2–15 letters are accepted; hyphenated and accented entries are excluded. Some entries are uncommon or dated, and proper-name filtering is imperfect.
